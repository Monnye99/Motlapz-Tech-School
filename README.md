# Motlapz-Tech-School
## Project goals and objectives
Website Proposal for Motlapz Tech School Hub

1. Organisation overview
Motlapz Tech School Hub is an IT training institute based in Pretoria, Gauteng. We were founded in 2025 to address the skills gap in South Africa's tech industry. We offer practical, industry-aligned courses in Software Development, Cyber Security, Networking, and IT Management to prepare students for careers in technology.

2. Mission, vision and target audience
Mission: To empower South African youth with in-demand IT skills through hands-on training and mentorship.
Vision: To become the leading tech skills hub in Gauteng, producing job-ready graduates by 2030.
Target Audience*: High school graduates, unemployed youth aged 18-35, career changers, and working professionals seeking IT certifications in Pretoria and surrounding areas.

3. Website goals and objectives
Primary Goal: Increase student applications by 70% in the first year.
Objectives:
 1. Provide clear information about courses, fees, and requirements.
 2. Allow prospective students to contact us and apply online.
 3. Build credibility and trust through FAQs and testimonials.
 4. Serve as a central hub for news, course updates, and announcements.

4. Key performance indicators (KPIs)
1. Website traffic: 2,000 monthly visitors by month 6
2. Lead conversion: 100 contact form submissions per month
3. Course page views: 70% of visitors view the Courses page
4. Bounce rate: Below 50%
5. Mobile users: 60% of traffic comes from mobile devices
5. Proposed features and functionality*
Course Catalog : Detailed pages for each of the 4 courses with duration and outcomes
Contact Form: Name, email, phone, course interest - sends to admin email
FAQ Section: Answers to common questions about fees, duration, certification
Responsive Design: Works on phone, tablet, and desktop
Apply Now Buttons: Links from Courses page to Contact page
Footer*: Address, phone, email, copyright info

6. Core services
1. Software Development: 12 Months - Python, Java, HTML, CSS, Web Apps
2. Cyber Security: 9 Months - Ethical Hacking, Network Security, Data Protection
3. Networking: 6 Months - CCNA, Server Setup, Routers and Switches
4. IT Management: 12 Months - IT Support, Project Management, Business Systems

7. Design and user experience*
- Style: Professional, modern, tech-focused. Colours: Red #E63946 and White
- UX: Simple 5-page navigation: Home | About | Services | Enquiries | Contact
- Layout: Clean cards for courses, clear headings, easy-to-read fonts
- Accessibility: Large buttons, alt text for images, fast loading
- Focus on mobile-first since most students will browse on phones.

8. Technical requirements
- Platform: Static HTML5, CSS3 website
- Hosting: Affordable shared hosting with cPanel
- Domain: http://www.motlapztech.co.za
- File Structure: 5 HTML pages + 1 CSS folder + Images folder
- Security: Basic form validation, no database needed for phase 1
- Analytics: Google Analytics to track visitors

9. Timeline and milestones
Phase | Duration | Deliverable
Planning & Content | week 2 | Sitemap, text and images collected
Design & HTML Build | Week 4-6 | All 5 pages coded with CSS
Testing | Week 8 | Check links, mobile view, fix bugs
Launch | Week 12 | Upload to hosting and go live
Total Project Duration: 3 months

10. Conclusion
This website is critical for Motlapz Tech School Hub to establish an online presence and attract students. A professional, simple website will make it easier for prospective students to find information and apply. By focusing on clear course info and easy contact, we will convert website visitors into enrolled students and support the school's growth goals.

PROPOSAL NO:2
Website Proposal for GreenBite Organic Foods

1. Organisation overview
GreenBite Organic Foods is a local Pretoria-based business that delivers fresh, organic fruits, vegetables, and meal kits directly to homes and offices. Established in 2024, we work with 15+ local farms to promote healthy eating and support sustainable agriculture.
2. Mission, vision and target audience
Mission: To make healthy, organic food accessible and convenient for everyone in Gauteng.
Vision: To become South Africa’s leading online organic grocery platform by 2030.
 Target Audience: Health-conscious adults aged 22-45, families, gym-goers, and working professionals in Pretoria, Centurion, and Midrand.

3. Website goals and objectives
Primary Goal: Increase online orders by 60% in the first 12 months.
Objectives:
 1. Allow customers to browse products and order online with delivery booking.
 2. Showcase farm partners and build trust.
 3. Collect customer data for email marketing and loyalty program.

4. Key performance indicators (KPIs)
1. Website traffic: 5,000 monthly visitors by month 6
2. Conversion rate: 4% of visitors place an order
3. Average order value: R450+
4. Bounce rate: Below 40%
5. Customer retention: 30% repeat orders

5. Proposed features and functionality
- Online product catalog with categories: Fruits, Vegetables, Meal Kits, Pantry
- Shopping cart + secure online payment: PayFast, Card, EFT
- Delivery scheduling calendar with address checker
- Customer accounts, order history, and loyalty points
- Blog with recipes and nutrition tips
- WhatsApp chat support button
- Mobile-responsive design

6. Core services
1. Weekly Organic Produce Boxes: Curated boxes delivered every Monday
2. Custom Meal Kits: Ingredients + recipe cards for 2-4 people
3. Corporate Catering: Healthy office fruit and snack delivery
4. Farm Tours & Events: Book online to visit partner farms

7. Design and user experience
Style: Clean, fresh, natural. Colours: Green #2A9D8F, Cream #FAF3E0
UX: Big product photos, 3-click checkout, filters for "vegan", "gluten-free"
Navigation: Home | Shop | Our Farms | Recipes | About | Contact
 Focus on fast loading and mobile-first since most orders come from phones.

8. Technical requirements
 Platform: WordPress + WooCommerce for easy product management
Hosting: Cloud hosting with SSL certificate
Payment Gateway: PayFast integration
SEO: Yoast SEO plugin, Google Analytics 4
Security: Daily backups, spam protection, GDPR-compliant forms

9. Timeline and milestones
Phase | Duration | Deliverable
Planning & Research | Week 1-2 | Sitemap, wireframes approved
Design | Week 3-4 | Homepage + Product page mockups
Development | Week 5-8 | Full site build + payment testing
Testing & Launch | Week 9 | Bug fixes, go live
Post-launch Support | Week 10-12 | SEO, training, updates
Total Project Duration: 9 Weeks

10. Budget estimate
Item | Estimated Cost ZAR
Domain + Hosting 1 year | R2,500
Website Design & Development | R18,000
E-commerce + Payment Setup | R5,000
SEO + Content Writing | R4,000
Maintenance 3 months | R3,000
Tota R32,500
11. Conclusion
This website will position GreenBite Organic Foods as a trusted, modern brand and create a new revenue channel through e-commerce. With online ordering and farm storytelling, we will build customer loyalty and grow beyond walk-in sales. We recommend launching before the January "healthy lifestyle" season to maximize impact.
